Latest Discoveries

Why Do We Dream?

Dreams are one of the most fascinating mysteries of the human brain.

Scientists believe dreams may be connected to memory, emotions and information processing.

The Mystery of Black Holes

Black holes are regions of space where gravity is extremely powerful.

Their gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape once it crosses the event horizon.

How Does the Brain Learn?

Our brain changes and creates connections when we learn something new.

Learning strengthens connections between neurons. This process helps us remember information and skills.

Why Is the Ocean Blue?

Have you ever wondered why the ocean appears blue?

Water absorbs many colors of light while blue light travels farther and is scattered back to our eyes.

Why Do We Get Déjà Vu?

Déjà vu is the strange feeling that you have experienced a moment before.

Researchers believe déjà vu may happen when the brain processes a new experience in a way that feels familiar.

Can Plants Communicate?

Plants may communicate with each other using chemical signals and underground networks.

Plants can release chemical signals and interact with fungi and other organisms around their roots.
